MyAniTrack 1.0.0
Developer: TairyFail
Support: tairyfailsupport@gmail.com

An independent anime and manga tracker for MyAnimeList. This is not the official MyAnimeList app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by MyAnimeList.

Data and content
MyAnimeList — account connection, lists, title information, released episodes and RSS content.
https://myanimelist.net/
Jikan — independent API providing additional anime/manga information.
https://jikan.moe/
