App name: MyAniTrack
Developer: TairyFail
Support: tairyfailsupport@gmail.com

Short description:
Track anime and manga, see new episodes and explore your personal statistics.

Full description:
MyAniTrack is an independent tracker for managing the anime and manga lists in your MyAnimeList account.

• Manage progress, ratings and list entries.
• Swipe between status tabs.
• See released episodes and countdowns for upcoming broadcasts.
• Discover titles, seasonal anime and rankings.
• Explore your status breakdown, ratings and most-watched genres.
• Choose English or Turkish, light or dark themes and multiple list layouts.
• Access cached lists offline and synchronize changes when connected.

A MyAnimeList account is required. Some content and airing information depends on third-party service availability. MyAniTrack does not stream or download videos. It is not the official MyAnimeList app and is not endorsed by MyAnimeList.
